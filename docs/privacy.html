1. Who we are

Vaultly is a mobile application that stores your receipts, warranties and subscriptions and reminds you before something expires or renews. This policy describes the app as it actually works.

Vaultly is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] (“Vaultly”, “we”, “us”). For the purposes of applicable data protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described below.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect and store:

  • Email address — used to identify your account, sign you in, and send account emails such as confirmation and email-change verification.
  • Password — if you register with email and password. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Google account identifier and email — if you choose “Continue with Google”. We receive your email address and a unique identifier. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Display name — optional, only if you set one.
  • Language, currency and reminder preferences.

2.2 Content you add to your vault

This is the information the app exists to hold. It is created by you, and belongs to you:

  • Receipts and invoices — merchant name, total amount, currency, purchase date, category, item name, notes, and the number of line items.
  • Warranties — expiry date, duration, and which reminders you want.
  • Subscriptions — service name, amount, billing period, next renewal date, and whether it renews automatically.
  • Receipt images — the photographs you take or select.

2.3 Purchase information

If you subscribe to Vaultly Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your card or payment details. We receive from our subscription provider only your subscription status and its expiry date, so the app knows whether Premium is active.

2.4 Technical information

  • Authentication tokens — stored on your device so you stay signed in.
  • Push notification token — stored only if notifications are enabled, so reminders can reach your device.

Reminders about expiring warranties and upcoming renewals are scheduled locally on your device. Their content is not sent to our servers.

3. What we do not collect

Vaultly contains no analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Advertising identifiers or any advertising profile
  • Location data
  • Contacts, calendar, call logs or SMS
  • Browsing or app-usage analytics
  • Your photo library beyond the specific images you choose to attach
  • Biometric data

Camera and photo-library permissions are used only at the moment you scan or attach a receipt. Vaultly does not access your camera or photos in the background.

5. Receipt scanning and AI processing

When you scan a receipt, the image is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI for text extraction. The extracted text is then analysed to identify the merchant, total, date, warranty term and similar fields, and those suggested values are shown to you for review before anything is saved.

  • The AI provider is used only to read the image you submitted.
  • The image is transmitted for processing; we do not store a copy of it on our AI processing servers. Your receipt image is stored only in your private storage area (see section 7).
  • Handling and retention by OpenAI are governed by OpenAI’s API terms and privacy policy. Under those terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI’s models. See openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • The scan is a suggestion, not a decision. Every extracted field is editable, and nothing is saved without your confirmation.

If you prefer not to use AI extraction, you can add every record manually and never scan a receipt.

7. How your data is stored and secured

  • Encrypted in transit. All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encrypted at rest. Our database and file storage encrypt stored data at rest.
  • Row-level isolation. Every record carries the identifier of the account that created it, and database security policies allow a signed-in user to read and write only their own rows. This is enforced by the database itself, not merely by the app.
  • Private image storage. Receipt images are kept in a private bucket, in a folder named after your account identifier. The bucket is not publicly readable. Access policies restrict every read, write and delete to your own folder.
  • Short-lived access links. When the app displays one of your receipt images, it requests a signed link that expires after a short period. There are no permanent public URLs to your images.
  • Secure sign-in. Authentication uses the PKCE flow, and session tokens are held in your device’s protected storage.
  • Secrets stay server-side. Keys for AI processing are held only on our server and are never included in the app.

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk.

8. Your ownership of your data

You own the content you put into Vaultly. Your receipts, receipt images, warranties, subscriptions and notes remain yours.

We claim no ownership of it. We do not use it to train AI models, we do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent it. We process it only to provide the app to you, and to comply with the law.

9. How long we keep data

  • While your account exists — your vault content is kept so it is there when you open the app.
  • When you delete an item — the record is removed from our database.
  • When you delete your account — your data is deleted as described in section 11.
  • Backups — residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

You can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything else — including a copy of your data — email us at appsupport49@gmail.com from the address registered to your account. We respond within 30 days.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • All receipt images stored for your account
  • All vault items, and the warranties and subscriptions attached to them
  • Your profile, preferences and any earned bonus slots
  • Your login credentials and authentication record

This action cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate and permanent, aside from residual copies in encrypted backups which are overwritten in the ordinary course.

If you cannot access the app, email appsupport49@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete the account for you.

Note: cancelling a paid subscription is handled through Google Play, not through us. Deleting your Vaultly account does not automatically cancel a Play subscription.
